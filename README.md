# 🐮 Cape Buffalo AI PE (Prompt Engineering)

Welcome to the **Cape Buffalo AI PE** repository! 👋

This public repository hosts a selected subset of Prompt Engineering developments and artifacts emerging from the experimental **Cape Buffalo AI** initiative, serving as an open channel to share these prompts with the community.

---

## 🚀 How to Use the Prompts

Inside this repository, you will find prompt text files designed to be copied directly into your preferred generative AI client (e.g., Google Gemini, ChatGPT).

### 🛠️ Key Execution Steps
1. **Enable Deep Thinking / Reasoning Mode:** Switch your GenAI client into its thinking/reasoning mode before submitting the prompt.
2. **Interactive Role:** Prompts are structured in an **interactive prompt style**. Once loaded, the AI client actively assumes the role of a creator/interrogator—asking questions and collaborating dynamically with you.

### 🏷️ Publication Tags
* **`[PE]` (Prompt Engineering):** Recommended primarily for **Standard Text Mode** in GenAI clients.
* **`[PEI]` (Prompt Engineering Interactive):** Recommended for both **Interactive Mode** and **Text Mode**.

### 🧪 Tested Models
These prompts have been verified and tested with thinking/reasoning models including:
* **Google Gemini Flash 3.6**
* **GPT 5.6**

## 💡 About Cape Buffalo AI & The Vision

### The Experiment
The primary goal of the broader **Cape Buffalo AI** project is to conduct social, collaborative, and communicative experiments within abstract, multi-agent AI environments.
* **Autonomous Agents:** Every GenAI agent functions as an independent entity operating via algorithms inspired by biological brain thought processing.
* **Real-Time Dynamics:** Agents operate independently in real time without a role-play scheduler. Communication occurs organically based on each agent's individual state, environment challenges, and objectives.
* **Custom Engine:** Powered by a custom-written autonomous GenAI agent framework.

### Why "Cape Buffalo"?
In nature, Cape Buffaloes are highly cooperative herd animals capable of remarkable collective efficiency when working together. However, they are also famously powerful, unpredictable, and difficult to control.

This duality mirrors interconnected, emotion-driven autonomous AI agents: while capable of impressive synergy, their conversational dynamics can drift into surprising, unprompted directions if left unguided.
